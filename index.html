Privacy Policy & Terms of Use - Borrow

Last updated: August 04, 2026

Important Warning: The Borrow application is a personal management tool. It in no way constitutes a legal source or legal proof. Use of the application is at your sole responsibility.

1. Data Collection & Google Drive

Borrow collects your email address only if you choose to use the Cloud backup function.

Google Drive Usage: If you enable Cloud backup, the app uses the Google Drive API only to read and write its own backup files in a dedicated hidden folder (AppData). The app has no access to any of your other personal files. No data from your Drive is transmitted to our servers.

2. Photos and Media

Photos taken via the app are stored locally on your device. They are also synchronized to your personal Google Drive space only if the backup function is enabled by you.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads provided by Google AdMob (except for Pro version users). Google AdMob may collect and use anonymous data (such as your device's advertising ID) to personalize ads and analyze audience, in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. User Rights (GDPR)

In accordance with data protection regulations, you can delete all your information at any time by deleting your records in the app or by signing out of your Google account. Your data is removed from our synchronization logs immediately upon your action.

Records created in Borrow are for information purposes only. They do not constitute a debt recognition, a contract, or a document with legal value. The publisher of Borrow disclaims all liability in the event of loss, theft, damage, or any dispute regarding the items listed.

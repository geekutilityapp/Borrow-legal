Privacy Policy of Borrow

Last updated: August 03, 2026

This privacy policy describes how your personal information is handled in the Borrow mobile application.

1. Data Collection

Borrow collects your email address only if you choose to use the Cloud Backup feature. Photos taken within the app are stored locally on your device and on your personal Google Drive space if backup is enabled.

2. Use of Data

Your data is used exclusively for the functioning of the application. We do not sell any data to third parties.

3. Advertising

The application uses Google AdMob. Users who upgrade to the Pro version will have all advertisements removed.

4. User Rights

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the records in the application or by signing out of your Google account.

5. Contact

For any questions, contact us via the app support.
